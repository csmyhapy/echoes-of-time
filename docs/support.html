Frequently asked questions

Is the app online? Does it need the internet?

No. Echoes of Time works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server. All your photos, voice notes, and text are saved only on your device.

Where is my memory book stored?

Everything stays in the app's private storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded, collected, or analysed. If you delete the app, its data is removed from the device.

I bought the Full Version but it isn't unlocked. What do I do?

Open Settings inside the app and choose Restore Purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; sign in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the app.

How do I export or share my memory book?

Use the export option to create a formatted PDF book or an archive package, then share it through the iOS share sheet (Files, Mail, AirDrop, etc.). Export is always started by you.

Can I keep older relatives' archives safe?

Switch to Reader mode for a large-text, large-button read-only view so family can browse and listen on their own. You can also lock the app with Face ID / Touch ID.

Report a problem or suggest a feature

Email support@lifememory.life with your device model, iOS version, and what happened. Feedback and feature ideas are welcome.
